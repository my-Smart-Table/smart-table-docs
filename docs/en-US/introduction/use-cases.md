# Use Cases

SmartTable is suitable for a wide range of business scenarios, from personal task management to enterprise-level project collaboration.

## Project Management

Use SmartTable to manage projects:

- Track project progress
- Assign tasks and owners
- Set deadlines and priorities
- Manage workflow through Kanban view

## Customer Relationship Management

Build a lightweight CRM system:

- Manage customer information
- Track sales opportunities
- Record customer communication history
- Analyze customer data

## Content Management

Manage content creation and publishing process:

- Track content creation progress
- Manage editing and publishing status
- Coordinate content calendar
- Analyze content performance

## Human Resources Management

Simplify HR daily work:

- Manage employee information
- Track recruitment process
- Record attendance and leave
- Manage training plans

## Inventory Management

Track products and inventory:

- Manage product catalog
- Monitor inventory levels
- Track order status
- Analyze sales data

## Personal Task Management

Organize personal work and life:

- Create to-do lists
- Track habit development
- Manage personal projects
- Plan schedules
